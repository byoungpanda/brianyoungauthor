Print. All paperbacks are set up with Ingram (IngramSpark) at the standard 55% wholesale discount and are returnable, so Ingram, Baker & Taylor, your regional wholesaler, or your local bookstore can order them by ISBN. Hardcover editions of Sophia Sprite and Pinky Whiskers exist but are sold through Amazon only. If you would rather buy direct, paperbacks are available through the store page, and signed copies are available by email.

Ebooks and audio. The Sophia Sprite ebook is distributed to libraries through OverDrive and Hoopla beginning November 23, 2026. The Pinky Whiskers and Sacred Vein ebooks are Amazon exclusives in Kindle Unlimited, so they are not on library platforms; the paperbacks are the library editions. The audiobook of Sophia Sprite, narrated by Audie Award winner Bahni Turpin, is on Apple Books, Amazon, and Audible only until the end of February 2027. It comes to libraries, and everywhere else audiobooks are sold, on March 1, 2027. Patrons can hear Chapter 1 free now. The Pinky Whiskers and Sacred Vein audiobooks are sold only on Amazon and Audible, so they are not on library platforms either.

Cataloging help. Suggested subject headings:

  • Sophia Sprite and the Sasquatch Secret: Sasquatch, Fiction; Grief, Fiction; Friendship, Fiction; Secrets, Fiction; Fantasy; Olympic Peninsula (Wash.), Fiction.
  • The Adventures of Pinky Whiskers and Sharp Nose: Mice, Fiction; Families, Fiction; Home, Fiction; Animals, Fiction.
  • The Sacred Vein series: Treasure troves, Fiction; Utah, Fiction; Ute Indians, Fiction; Uinta Mountains (Utah), Fiction; Historians, Fiction.

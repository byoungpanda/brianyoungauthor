Story angles

  • 129 agents said no. Brian queried 129 literary agents, published the book himself, and hired an Audie Award winner to narrate it.
  • An Audible Hall of Famer on a self-published book. Bahni Turpin, narrator of The Hate U Give and Children of Blood and Bone, performs all 9 hours and 39 minutes.

  • A Chicago book hunt. Ten paperbacks are hidden in Little Free Libraries across the city for kids to find, read, and pass along.

About the author

Author bio

Short bio

Brian Young is a novelist who believes the best stories live in the spaces between what we know and what we imagine. He grew up in Utah exploring the High Uintas, the setting of his Sacred Vein historical adventure series, and spent eight years hiking the Pacific Northwest, which inspired the Sophia Sprite series for young readers. He is also the author of The Adventures of Pinky Whiskers and Sharp Nose, an illustrated chapter book drawn from stories his great-grandfather used to tell. He lives in Chicago.

Long bio

Brian Young is a novelist who believes the best stories live in the spaces between what we know and what we imagine.

Brian grew up in Utah hiking, camping, and searching the High Uintas, the same mountains that provide the dramatic backdrop for The Sacred Vein. Those formative years exploring wilderness trails and hearing legends of lost Spanish gold planted the seeds of the Sacred Vein series, a historical adventure saga that blends treasure hunting, indigenous rights, and the wild landscapes of the American West.

He spent eight years in Seattle, where he developed a deep love of hiking the trails of the Pacific Northwest. The misty woods, the sense that something ancient might be watching from just beyond the tree line, the way the wilderness holds secrets it isn't ready to share: all of it found its way onto the page. Those years hiking the PNW trails became the inspiration for the Sophia Sprite series.

When he's not writing, Brian can be found tending to his collection of Funko Pops, watching the Chicago Sky in the WNBA, and working on home improvement projects that always seem to take longer than planned. He currently lives in Chicago with a deep appreciation for a good story, a well-crafted sentence, and the possibility that there's more to the world than meets the eye.

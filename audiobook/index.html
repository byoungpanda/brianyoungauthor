The narrator

Bahni Turpin

Bahni Turpin has won nine Audie Awards and was inducted into the Audible Narrator Hall of Fame in 2018. Her credits include The Hate U Give, Children of Blood and Bone, and The Underground Railroad.

In Sophia Sprite and the Sasquatch Secret she gives distinct voices to a cast that includes a leprechaun several centuries old, a seven-foot Northborn warrior, a Seattle teenager who has just lost everything, and the villain who took it from her.

“Hearing Bahni Turpin bring Sophia to life is the closest I’ll ever come to seeing the movie in my head.”

Brian Young
